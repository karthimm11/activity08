Every player has a part

Roles on the pitch

Different skills, one team. These are the key roles that shape a cricket match.

01 / BAT

Batter

Builds the innings, scores runs, and takes on the bowling attack.

02 / BOWL

Bowler

Delivers the ball, takes wickets, and works to keep the scoring down.

03 / ALL ROUND

All-rounder

Contributes with both bat and ball to give the team extra options.

04 / KEEP

Wicketkeeper

Guards the stumps, catches edges, and supports the fielding side.
